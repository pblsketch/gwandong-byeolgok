Wide landscape background painting for a 2D side-scrolling game. Absolutely no text, no letters, no calligraphy, no seals or stamps, no signboards, no people. Side view composition. Keep the bottom 22% of the image calm, low-detail mist or water (it will be covered by the game's ground). Main interest in the middle band.
