Character portrait sheet for a Korean visual novel: a 2 by 2 grid on a solid flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background, each cell a bust portrait (head and shoulders, facing slightly right) of the SAME man, Jeong Cheol, a Joseon-dynasty scholar-official in his mid-40s: slim face, thin mustache and small goatee, black gat hat with a wide brim, pale jade-green dopo robe with a red sash. Style: Korean ink-wash illustration with watercolor color and clean line art. Expressions: top-left calm and dignified; top-right deeply moved with awe, looking up; bottom-left sorrowful and longing, looking into the distance; bottom-right resolute and determined. Each portrait separated by wide magenta gaps. No text.
